What Axone is

Axone is the independent equity research of Rezi Sabashvili. Every name is valued the way the sell side does it — DCF, trading comps, precedent transactions and a football-field synthesis — built entirely from public filings.

Methodology

How every name is valued

Three independent methods, triangulated into one target.

Discounted cash flow

Unlevered FCF discounted at WACC; terminal value cross-checked via Gordon growth and an exit EV/EBITDA multiple. EBIT = Profit from Recurring Operations, taxed at a normalized 25%.

WACC build-up

Cost of equity via CAPM (risk-free, levered beta, ERP), blended with after-tax cost of debt at target structure. Net debt on an IFRS-16 basis.

Trading comparables

EV/EBITDA and EV/Sales against a curated peer set, benchmarked to the peer median to isolate the embedded discount or premium.

Precedent transactions

Control-deal multiples from comparable sector M&A, setting a takeout floor alongside the standalone DCF.

Sum-of-the-parts

For multi-division groups, each business is valued on the EV/EBIT of its own listed peers, then summed to an enterprise value and bridged to equity, which makes any conglomerate discount explicit.

Banks: dividend discount & P/TBV vs RoTE

Deposits and debt are a bank's raw material, so EV, WACC and FCFF do not apply. Value comes from distributable capital: an excess-capital DDM at the CAPM cost of equity, a justified P/TBV = (RoTE − g) / (Ke − g), and comps and precedents read on P/TBV against RoTE.

The blended target weights three methods, adapted to each business model, with a two-way sensitivity grid (discount rate × terminal assumption) published for every name.
